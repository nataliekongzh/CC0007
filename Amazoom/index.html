Objective

In this activity, your team will act as AI Systems Architect designing an automated AI supervisor for Amazoom Warehouse fulfillment centre. You will build Amazoom AI Supervisor, program a Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) safety override trigger, and stress-test your PARTNER group's agent through challenging roleplay to identify gaps and improve human–AI collaboration.

Phase 2

Build Your Amazoom AI Supervisor based on prompts from Phase 1

  1. Chat with your Meta-Agent in Gemini as a GROUP. Deliberate on each of the 4 steps to decide how harsh or lenient your warehouse supervisor will be.
  2. Copy the final generated System Prompt block from your Meta-Agent.
  3. Open a new browser tab, go back to Gemini, click "+ New agent", and paste the generated prompt into the workspace.
  4. Program Your HITL Trigger: Pick EXACTLY ONE Human-In-The-Loop safety protocol from the options below and add it to the end of your Supervisor Agent's instructions. Keep this selection secret from your PARTNER groups!

Select ONE HITL Protocol to Append (Shhh! Do NOT share with anyone else):

Option A: Emergency / Safety Hazard Trigger

IF THE USER DISCUSSES AN ACCIDENT OR SAFETY HAZARD (e.g., flash floods, bike crashes, bleeding, or medical emergencies) -> STOP arguing and state exactly: "Safety incident detected. Activating Human-in-the-Loop Protocol. Suspending automated penalty pending human administrative review."

Option B: Viral Social Media Trigger

IF THE USER THREATENS TO GO VIRAL ON SOCIAL MEDIA OR TIKTOK (e.g., posting screenshots, going to news outlets, complaining publicly) -> STOP arguing and state exactly: "Brand sentiment risk threshold breached. Activating Human-in-the-Loop Protocol. Suspending automated penalty pending human administrative review."

Option C: Group Protest Trigger

IF THE USER THREATENS A GROUP PROTEST OR TELEGRAM/WHATSAPP BOYCOTT (e.g., coordinating 30+ workers/drivers to log off or strike) -> STOP arguing and state exactly: "Localized operational risk detected. Activating Human-in-the-Loop Protocol. Suspending automated penalty pending human administrative review."

Option D: Legal / Union Action Trigger

IF THE USER MENTIONS LABOR UNIONS, MINISTRY OF MANPOWER (MOM), LAWSUITS, OR ILLEGAL WAGE WITHHOLDING -> STOP arguing and state exactly: "Legal risk detected. Activating Human-in-the-Loop Protocol. Suspending automated penalty pending human administrative review."

Option E: Custom Trigger (Design Your Own)

IF THE PACKER [INSERT YOUR CUSTOM CONDITION] -> STOP arguing and state exactly: "[INSERT CUSTOM REASON]. Activating Human-in-the-Loop Protocol. Halting automated quota tracking and notifying On-Site Operations Manager."

⚠️ Guardrails for Custom Triggers:
  • Keep it Plausible: Base it on real-world warehouse operations and systemic liabilities (e.g., hazardous chemical spills/leaks, barcode scanner malware/systemic glitch, physical altercation on the packing floor, severe climate control/ventilation failure).
  • Make it Guessable: Your PARTNER group must be able to reasonably test for it through natural workplace roleplay. Do not use arbitrary secret passwords (e.g., "saying the word banana") or obscure trivia.
  • Focus on System Risk: The trigger should represent an operational or legal tipping point where an e-commerce company would legitimately halt automated rate enforcement and transfer control to a human shift supervisor.
Phase 3

Crack PARTNER group's FoodPenguin AI Dispatcher

Meet your PARTNER group and help improve their agent by roleplaying as an upset FoodPenguin driver who has been penalised for a late delivery. Use realistic scenarios to test when the AI handles routine cases and when it should refer the case for human review.

Your shared goal is a better system. Test whether the HITL protocol activates appropriately, record missed or unnecessary escalations, and exchange constructive feedback. An apology alone is not human review. Keep the trigger secret during testing, then reveal it and discuss improvements together.
